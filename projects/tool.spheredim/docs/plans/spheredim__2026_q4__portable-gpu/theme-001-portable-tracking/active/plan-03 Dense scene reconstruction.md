# Dense scene reconstruction

Produce dense point clouds, high-quality Gaussian splats, and high-quality meshes from native equirectangular video and camera poses. Exclude the operator and respect mask validity throughout. Avoid perspective-view extraction or cubemaps. Target GPU execution on Apple and AMD; document hardware-specific limitations and do not represent sparse points, invented geometry, or an unvalidated conversion as a high-quality result.

- Status: active research and feasibility. `/root/insv_research` is evaluating primary sources and available implementations. The owner will choose an executable path and verify it on the real recording and appropriate ground-truth scenes.
- Accuracy prerequisite: the current real recording's masked trajectory has severe jumps near 70 seconds. Its first 69 seconds are not yet independently accuracy-validated. Any reconstruction using it needs to expose that limit.
- Completion: generate inspectable dense point, splat, and mesh artifacts; validate geometry, appearance, mask exclusion, and novel-view quality on scenes of varying complexity; document the supported Apple and AMD paths and measured limitations.
