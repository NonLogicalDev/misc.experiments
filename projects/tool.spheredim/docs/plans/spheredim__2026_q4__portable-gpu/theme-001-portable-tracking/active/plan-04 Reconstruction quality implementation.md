## Verified evidence and limits

1. The corrected 138-frame mask experiment used 1024×512 panoramas and identical SfM settings. Tight masks gave 134 registered images in one component versus 124 in two components for the broad mask; retained features increased from 106,119 to 191,300. This supports the mask change on this sample. It does not prove pose accuracy or cover the intervening frames or final seconds.
2. The reviewed tight exclusion removes a median 11.62% of spherical area. Adding a 32-pixel feature guard increases exclusion to 18.19%. These are different masks serving different purposes. Preserve that distinction; do not expand the visual or training mask merely to protect descriptors.
3. Spirula applies the SfM mask after extraction and checks feature centers. Masking input pixels before extraction prevents descriptors from observing correctly excluded operator pixels. Some descriptor supports still touch artificial boundaries, even with a fixed guard. The first A/B placed the blackout boundary at the guard edge and was invalid; only corrected `-v2` results count.
4. One SfM pose at frame 1785 (59.5595 s) has large adjacent rotation disagreement with DVO and the fused stream. The original model remains untouched. A derived training set excludes it and lists four unregistered frames (1200, 1305, 1500, 1560). Avoid proving quality by deleting difficult intervals without reporting coverage.
5. Initial equal 3,000-step pose comparison on 133 eligible frames, 116 training and 17 held out, reports 21.329 dB / 0.774 SSIM for SfM versus 19.088 / 0.717 for DVO. Both use the same SfM seed, so initialization favors the SfM geometry; this is a practical pipeline comparison, not an unbiased isolation of all pose effects. Holdout images contributed to poses/seed. Both can still look soft. The paired renders, full checkpoints and reports were preserved beside the recording with verified SHA-256 copies. SfM wins PSNR on 15/17 and SSIM on 17/17 frames. Both arms enabled trainable 9×9 calibration warp from step 1000 and blur-tolerant SSIM, so their projectors are not fixed during training.
6. Existing synthetic 360DVO results fail the original image target: loop 1.669%, dynamic 1.270%, low texture 32.791% worst-frame image p95; low-texture trajectory also fails. Real same-footage estimator agreement is not ground truth. No AMD hardware validation exists.

Extend the existing app-free `scripts/stitch_insv.py` / pinned insta360-rs workflow; do not introduce a second unrelated exporter. Check per-lens timestamps, calibration, lens center separation, housing/profile selection, motion and physical rolling shutter. Distinguish absent coverage from inconsistent measurements in overlap. A depth-independent warp cannot in general reconcile nearby and distant objects from different lens centers.

The output contract is an observation panorama plus aligned sidecars: per-pixel validity, source lens/coordinates, actual contribution weights and, where required, ray origin/direction. Preserve raw sources and calibration/pose/PTS. Reprojection and ordinary resampling of observed pixels are permitted; inpainting, stretching or unconstrained flow that makes unsupported content appear measured is not. Do not use a visually smooth blend of incompatible surfaces as geometric evidence. Prefer a deterministic valid lens observation where overlap disagrees, record its ray provenance, and mark uncertain residual regions separately. Keep usable overlap rather than assuming a fixed wide band is missing.

The current central-camera SfM backend cannot automatically consume multiple ray origins. Therefore deliver two explicit levels: a conservative central-panorama baseline with measured disagreement excluded, and a later generalized-ray path only after its estimator and renderer support per-lens origins. Do not claim a lens-provenance sidecar alone corrects the central camera approximation.

Generate validity after the exact stabilization/seam/readout mapping applied to each frame, with yaw retained. Maintain separate masks for absent source support, stitch uncertainty and operator exclusion. In invalid regions a display fill is allowed only with zero validity; those pixels and their interpolation/filter footprints contribute nothing to feature extraction or training. The reference viewer should be able to show the support/uncertainty overlay, without silently presenting display fill as measured content.

Acceptance: every valid output sample traces to a valid raw lens footprint; synthetic injected blind wedges never become valid through blending; excluded RGB changes cannot affect valid supervision; source timestamps and horizon-lock rotation also transform validity correctly; the doorframe example is either correctly projected with provenance or narrowly invalidated, never cosmetically smeared. Check at multiple scene depths and adjacent times to catch parallax and temporal wobble. Dynamic/optical-flow seam modes are research diagnostics only until their pixel displacement is represented in the reconstruction ray model and validated; a prettier export is not the success criterion.

### 4. Add confidence-filtered spherical depth on GPU

Adapt the existing CPU native-sphere prototype into a verified GPU path, preferably compatible with the existing Vulkan stack or a small Rust/wgpu module. Use full ERP rays and radial distance, seam-aware matching, coarse-to-fine inverse-depth/range search, multiview visibility and occlusion checks. Start with geometric multiview evidence. Niantic MVSAnywhere/DoubleTake motivate adaptive depth ranges and geometry guidance, but their perspective networks and restricted licenses are not drop-in dependencies. If adding a learned panorama depth prior, separately audit weight license, native spherical input behavior, per-frame scale consistency and Apple/AMD execution. Treat it as a low-confidence prior, never fabricated measured depth.

Begin at 512×256, 32–64 inverse-depth hypotheses and 2–4 source views with bounded GPU buffers; profile before scaling to 2048×1024. Confidence must combine photometric uniqueness, at least two agreeing source views and forward/backward radial reprojection with visibility tests. Low-texture cases may abstain and must report lost coverage.
