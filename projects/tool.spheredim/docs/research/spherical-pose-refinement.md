# Spherical pose-refinement investigation

A native equirectangular SfM experiment on 120 masked panoramas yielded two disconnected components with 37 and 55 images and 28 unregistered images. Both used COLMAP EQUIRECTANGULAR cameras. Matching-based bridge and growth tests did not connect them. Reprojection error alone does not establish a correct physical trajectory.

A separate diagnostic triangulated 5,692 feature tracks from 879 verified full-panorama image pairs using the original 360DVO poses. All 120 cameras had observations, but after reserving 10% of tracks and removing tracks with fewer than three observations or less than two degrees of parallax, the remaining observation graph split into 113- and 7-camera components. Connectivity depends on weak tracks under this filter.

A fixed-intrinsics spherical bundle-adjustment test lowered its own objective but did not converge and repeatedly reported non-positive-definite factorization. An initial withheld-point comparison fixed withheld geometry to the original trajectory and therefore unfairly penalized geometry changes. With each withheld track independently re-triangulated under the compared poses, mean/median/p95 reprojection was 1.125/0.728/3.438 pixels for the original poses, 0.798/0.449/2.841 for unconstrained adjustment, and 0.865/0.505/2.960 with every tenth camera held fixed. These are track-fit checks, not independent physical ground truth. Relative orientation comparisons against estimated IMU motion also did not establish that the refined poses were better. The adjusted poses were not adopted.

Next, validate explicit gauge fixing and robust or soft-prior bundle adjustment on full-equirectangular synthetic scenes with known poses, independent holdouts, noise, occlusions, poles and seams. Require numerical convergence and accuracy checks before applying it to footage. Continue to preserve the original poses and label incomplete results.

A soft position-prior sweep on the real data used the original 360DVO camera centers as regularization, not truth. Priors of 0.01, 0.1 and 0.3 in arbitrary track units all produced non-positive-definite warnings and did not converge in 60 iterations; extending the 0.1 case to 500 iterations still did not converge. Re-triangulated held-out mean reprojection improved from 1.125 to approximately 0.78 pixels, but relative rotation agreement with the estimated IMU did not improve. Neither measurement proves physical accuracy. The candidates were not promoted. Position priors alone did not resolve the solver failure; the next work is to diagnose conditioning and implement calibrated robust relative-rotation priors, then verify them on synthetic known-truth and disconnected cases before testing real footage.
