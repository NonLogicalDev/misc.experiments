# Reconstruction quality report

Updated 2026-11-16. The best measured reconstruction so far is the 7,000-step continuation of the dense physical-lens rig model. It improves the pooled error over the 6,000-step model, but the reconstruction remains visibly soft in fine details and has not met the requested twofold quality target. This report is a current decision record; update its evidence and incumbent when a new trial is verified.

## Quality target and measurement

The fixed target anchor is the dense 6,000-step model: achieve at least a 50% reduction in pooled spherical weighted mean squared RGB error at matched source captures, and show visible fine-detail improvement. Every new candidate must also be compared directly with the current 7,000-step incumbent. For the current masks and views, half of the 6,000-step error is 0.00193296 on the 36 held-out views and 0.00413972 on the 10 selected training views. Numerical gains alone do not prove recovered detail.

The table pools squared RGB error across all common valid pixels using spherical area weights before dividing by total weight. Lower is better; these are not averages of per-image PSNR. Both models in each pair use the same source image and mask intersection without fitted image alignment or appearance correction. Each reconstruction is rendered at its own estimated pose for the same physical capture. The 36 splat-held-out images contributed to SfM, so they are not independent pose ground truth. The 10 selected training captures are a diagnostic, not a representative sample of every training view.

These are training clocks, not end-to-end times, and differing inputs and schedules prevent attributing the difference to one change.

## Input and pose decisions

Use the two synchronized physical lens observations as full equirectangular images, with fixed rig extrinsics and one body pose per timestamp. This preserves the separate lens observations and their physical support masks. Earlier stitched-panorama work uses different source observations and is not a controlled quantitative comparison to the physical rig. The recording's effective lens baseline and metric scale are not independently established.

The controlled explicit-pair SfM comparison increased sampling from 138 timestamps / 276 images to 412 timestamps / 824 images. The baseline graph has 37,950 candidate pairs; the dense graph retains those and adds temporal and synchronized pairs for 48,666 total. All images registered in each corresponding model. Measured SfM wall times were 61.86 s and 108.33 s respectively; this is not a runtime improvement, and excludes other stages. The runs use different prior-factor counts, and their diagnostic inputs retain a small distant person in the final seven cam0 frames. Shared-view training reprojection and orientation agreement are diagnostics rather than independent truth. ## ERP seam correction

The native ERP renderer clipped seam-crossing splats. The disabled [periodic raster layer](../../vendor/spirula-studio/layers/0006-erp-periodic-raster/) passed 16 GPU forward comparisons against CPU references and 28 ordinary-case finite-difference gradient checks; non-ERP pinhole controls also passed the stated tolerance. A complete source-built RGB trainer included all 82 affected variants and passed the full registry audit. On the same unmodified 6,000-step checkpoint, held-out spherical MSE fell 0.19% overall and 19.72% in the outer 16-pixel seam bands, while the 10 training views rose 0.69% overall and 14.20% in those bands. The correction remains disabled: fixture correctness does not establish a reconstruction-quality gain, and the separate meshing path, CUDA, and AMD remain unverified. See the [detailed seam note](spirula-erp-periodic-layer.md) and [checkpoint comparison](/private/tmp/spheredim-seam-eval-6k/report.json).

A 450,000-capacity resume from the 8,000-step model first rendered all 36 views byte-identically after host checkpoint adaptation. In a guarded 100-step smoke, the first refinement relocated 18,034 splats and added 11,966; the final state held 311,966 active splats in 450,000 slots. Held-out spherical MSE increased from 0.00387580 to 0.00405919 (4.73% worse), and pixel MSE increased 5.03%. The subsequent 2,000-update trial from the original 8,000-step checkpoint reached 450,000 active splats, with refinement stopped for the final 500 updates. Held-out spherical MSE increased from 0.00387580 to 0.00399190 (3.00% worse), while pixel MSE increased 3.38%; training-view detail was not audited. The trial is not promoted. ## Higher-resolution work and limits

Full 2048×1024 physical-lens preparation is complete: 824 images across 412 timestamps, about 1.03 GB of unique output. Independent checks verified all ordered pose rows, seed points, ERP metadata scaling and the same 36 holdouts. Downsampling those 36 references by 2×2 area averaging exactly reproduces prior RGB on common valid pixels. No 2048 training has started. A native 4096×2048 export pilot covers three timestamps and both lenses. Area-reduced 4096 exports differ from 2048 exports by 0.59–0.92 RGB levels mean absolute difference on valid intersections; this includes resampling and color effects and is not a sharpness measurement. The [pilot report](</Users/nonlogical/Downloads/SphereDim-VID_20181001_170023_00_104/native-resolution-4096-pilot-20261116/comparison.json>) records source hashes. The [resolution-resume audit](../../validation/spirula-seam-build/resolution-resume.md) specifies dataset and zero-step checks before a bounded continuation.

Keep total SphereDim task storage within 15 GB, with non-reconstruction support targeted to 1–2 GB and capped at 2 GB, while maintaining 35 GiB free. These are simultaneous constraints; do not treat an artifact's apparent size as recoverable space when files are shared. Runtime goals include more sampled frames and substantially lower total time, but the quality target and full pipeline measurements remain unmet. Preserve original inputs, active viewer state, checkpoints, and the best verified model.

The source is pinned through [Spirula layers](../../vendor/spirula-studio/layers/), including `0002-rotation-priors`, `0003-final-prior-refinement`, and `0007-explicit-sfm-pairs`; experimental `0004-zero-gradient-tiles` and `0006-erp-periodic-raster` are not promoted. Compare pixels with [compare_splat_pixels.py](../../tools/native-sfm/compare_splat_pixels.py). Private data and generated images stay outside the repository.

## First 2048 training check

The 7k checkpoint resumed for 100 updates at 2048×1024 with unchanged poses, camera identity, masks, 300,000 splat capacity and frozen refinement. Training took 15.946 s; the guarded run including 36-view evaluation took 36.72 s. Against the same checkpoint rendered at 2048, spherical MSE rose from 0.00393970 to 0.00403778 (+2.49%) and raw pixel MSE rose 2.77%. Masks and support are identical. This short trial is not promoted; a continuation to 1,000 total updates is in progress because 100 updates sample only a fraction of the 788 training cameras. The original 7k model remains preserved and displayed. See `rig-dense-native-2048-20261116/smoke-comparison.json` beside the recording.
