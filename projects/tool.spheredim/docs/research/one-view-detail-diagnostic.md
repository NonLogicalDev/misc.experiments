# One-view detail diagnostic

This experiment tests whether the existing 7k splat geometry can fit one actual training capture when only its color coefficients are optimized. It does not produce a reconstructed scene or evaluate generalization.

The selected source is original 2048 `train_001500_cam0.png`, whose raw 7k rendering already exists in `training-audit-7000` at evaluation index 22. The same original source image, mask and camera pose are linked once as training input and once as an explicitly diagnostic evaluation input. The original 36 heldouts and the preserved 7k checkpoint remain untouched.

Initialize from the preserved 7k PLY, with no checkpoint resume and no added seed points. Disable camera-indexed bilateral grid and PPISP, calibration correction and evaluation alignment. Set means, scale, quaternion, opacity, projective depth and noise learning rates to zero; disable shape regularizers, refinement and screen/world-size clipping. Keep the existing SSIM/L1 loss and SH/DC learning rates. First perform zero training updates, render the diagnostic image and require identical camera/source/mask/transform and at most one RGB level difference in no more than 0.01% of valid pixels against the saved raw 7k training-view audit before fitting. A 500-update fit may proceed only after that gate is reviewed.

For direct geometry evidence, use final-only compact saves: zero phase `steps-per-save=1`, fit phase `steps-per-save=500`, both with full optimizer save disabled. Compare zero and fit exported PLY geometry fields exactly and report any difference. Preserve per-field hashes and vertex counts before retiring the new zero-phase PLY and inference state; keep its config, render and metrics. Original PLY and checkpoints are never removed. A watchdog uses SIGKILL on timeout or budget violation so an interrupted run cannot write an unplanned full checkpoint. Budget is 160 MB total output and 120 seconds per phase, in addition to the 15 GB task cap, 2 GB support cap and 35 GiB free-space reserve.

The import gate passed: at most one RGB level differed in 12 of 1,000,652 valid pixels. Means, scales and opacity remained bitwise equal after 500 color-only updates, but all quaternion field hashes changed. An approved zero replay reproduced the retired zero PLY hash exactly; comparison measured maximum orientation drift of 0.00013260 degrees and p99 0.00001053 degrees. The strict bitwise geometry gate therefore failed, although the measured rotation drift was very small. The optimizer normalizes quaternions even when their learning rate is zero.

For this training capture, spherical MSE fell from 0.00940974 at zero import to 0.00112484 after color-only fitting. The RGB loss was still falling at step 500, so this does not establish convergence or a representation limit. A separate fit enabled the original means, scale, rotation and opacity learning rates, still without refinement or noise; its spherical MSE was 0.00036418 after 500 updates. The matched crop retained less window detail than the source. Both fits evaluated the same capture they optimized and neither establishes multi-view reconstruction fidelity. The input hashes, per-field geometry evidence, crops and measurements are retained with the local diagnostic outputs.

A follow-up fit used nine neighboring actual cam0 training captures and excluded frame 1500 from the new fit. Frames 1440 and 1560 remained original heldouts; frame 1500 had already been used in the original 7k training. All three zero-import renders passed the same diagnostic import tolerance and matched their original camera, source, mask and scene transform. After 1,000 updates, spherical MSE changed from 0.00250051 to 0.00455028 for 1440, 0.00940974 to 0.00468603 for 1500, and 0.00396922 to 0.00341856 for 1560. This mixed result does not establish a reconstruction-quality improvement. The heldout 1440 regression and the streaked window in the matched crop limit the interpretation; outdoor foliage may also move between captures. Exact commands, hashes, per-frame metrics and static clock/poster and window crops remain in the local diagnostic output.
